Silence Slicer — Preview Transport Update

- Keeps the external footage preview compact at 480x270.
- The preview remains a normal Windows window with its title bar, so it can be dragged anywhere on screen.
- Adds in-app transport controls on the Selected Moment tab:
  - Back 5 seconds
  - Play / Pause
  - Forward 5 seconds
  - Horizontal scrubber
- The timer, scrubber, and preview position stay synchronized.
- Seeking restarts ffplay at the requested point inside the selected preview range.
