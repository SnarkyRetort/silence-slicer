Silence Slicer - PCM chunk audio hardening

Changes:
- Temporary chunk files remain MKV.
- Temporary audio changed from FLAC to PCM signed 16-bit little-endian.
- Temporary PCM is normalized to 48 kHz stereo for predictable chunk compatibility.
- Final output behavior is unchanged: MP4/MKV selection is based on the chosen output filename, and final audio is encoded by the existing join stage.
- Existing tolerant damaged-input retry remains in place and now also uses PCM for its temporary chunk.

Why:
Some recordings caused FFmpeg's FLAC encoder to fail with "invalid block size" during chunk rendering. PCM avoids that encoder path entirely while remaining lossless for the temporary stage.
